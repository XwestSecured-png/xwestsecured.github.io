## 1. Purpose
This policy protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the organization's information. That includes client, donor and employee data, and the systems that process it. It sets the governance structure that all other security policies and standards follow.

## 2. Scope
This policy covers all workforce members (employees, volunteers, interns, contractors), all organization-owned or managed devices, cloud services, and any personal device used to access organizational data, at every site.

## 3. Governance & roles (GV.RR)
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board / Finance Committee | Approves risk appetite; receives quarterly security KPI report (GV.OV) |
| Executive Director | Accountable for the security program; accepts risks above appetite |
| IT Director (security lead) | Owns this policy, the risk register, the CSF profile and incident response |
| Department / Site managers | Ensure staff follow policies; approve access for their teams; own departmental risks |
| All workforce | Follow policies, complete training, report incidents and suspicious activity immediately |

## 4. Risk management (GV.RM)
- Cyber risk is managed through the **risk register** using a 5×5 likelihood × impact scale.
- **Risk appetite:** residual risk scores of **6 or below** are acceptable. Anything higher requires a mitigation plan or a documented acceptance signed by the Executive Director, with an expiry date of no more than 12 months.
- The CSF 2.0 Current and Target Profiles are reassessed every year.

## 5. Policy framework
This policy is supported by the following policies, each with its own owner:
Acceptable Use · Access Control · Data Classification & Handling · Vendor Risk Management · Incident Response · Backup & Recovery · Vulnerability & Patch Management · Security Awareness Training.

## 6. Minimum security requirements
1. Phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication for administrators, remote access and email (see the Access Control Policy).
2. Endpoint protection (EDR) and full-disk encryption on all managed laptops and desktops.
3. Security patches applied within policy SLAs. Vulnerabilities on the CISA KEV list are fixed within 7 days.
4. Backups follow the 3-2-1 rule, with at least one immutable or offline copy. Restores are tested at least quarterly.
5. Security logs are centralized and kept for at least one year.
6. Every workforce member completes security awareness training at onboarding and every year after.
7. Vendors that handle Confidential data are assessed before contract signing and reviewed every year.

## 7. Compliance & exceptions
Requests for an exception to this policy are submitted to the IT Director. Each request must state the business justification, the compensating controls and an expiry date. Exceptions are recorded in the risk register. Violations may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 8. Related obligations
Applicable laws and contracts include state data breach notification laws, HIPAA (if protected health information is handled), PCI DSS (if payment cards are processed), and grant or funder data requirements. Legal counsel confirms which apply.
